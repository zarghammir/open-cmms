# 11. Users and roles

Users and roles is where an administrator sees who uses WorkAxis and what each
role may do. Roles are the security model: every screen and every action asks
the role first, so this screen is the rule book.

This is the only module open to the administrator alone.

## What is on the screen

**The page heading:** **Users and roles**, then "Who uses WorkAxis and what
each role may do." An **Invite a person** button sits at the top right.

**The people table**, headed "The people in the organization, with the site each
works out of."

Columns are Name, Employee number, Role, Home site, Status, Email, and Trades.
Status is **Active** or **Inactive**. Trades are General maintenance; Heating,
ventilation and air conditioning; Electrical; Controls and instrumentation; and
Inspection. An auditor's row is marked "reads only".

Above it is the usual toolbar: a search box, "Name, employee number, email or
site", filter buttons, **Columns**, **Clear filters**, and a count.

**The role table**, headed **What each role may do**, described as "The same
rule the buttons obey, read across. A tick is a thing that role can do; a blank
is a control they see, disabled, with the reason."

It has one row per action and one column per role. Chapter 15 reproduces it in
full.

## Find a person

1. Type into the search box. It matches the name, the employee number, the
   email and the site.
2. Or filter by role, by site or by status.
3. **Clear filters** starts again.

## Check what a role may do

1. Scroll to **What each role may do**.
2. Find the action down the left — for example "Call a job off".
3. Read across to the role's column. A tick means that role may do it. A dash
   means they will see the control, switched off, with the reason.

A role that may change nothing at all is marked **Reads only** at the head of
its column. The auditor is the one such role.

This table is not written by hand. It is generated by asking the same rule every
button in the product asks, so it cannot drift out of step with what the buttons
actually do.

## Invite a person

You cannot, yet. The **Invite a person** button is on the screen and switched
off. Move to it or hover over it and it says "Invitations arrive with real
sign-in, in Phase 2."

It is left visible on purpose, so you can see the feature is planned rather than
missing. Note that this button is switched off for everybody, including the
administrator: it is the phase that is holding it, not your role.

Picture: `docs/screenshots/WA-22/en-users-invite-reason.png`

## Change somebody's role

Not on this screen, in this phase. There is no sign-in yet, so there are no
accounts to move between roles.

What you can do instead is look at the product as a different role: open the
session menu at the top right and pick one. The opener of this manual has the
steps. Every screen, every button and every list then behaves as that role, which
is the point of the switch.

## What each role can do here

Users and roles is open to the **administrator** only.

For the other five roles it does not appear in the module list at all. If one of
them opens the address, the screen says **Not available to your role**, then
"This screen is limited to other roles. Your current role is" and the role name,
with "Switch role from the session menu to see it" underneath.

Within the screen, an administrator may read both tables. Neither table can be
edited in this phase, and the only button on the screen is the switched-off
invitation described above.
