# 9. Documents

Documents is the library: manuals, certificates, drawings and reports, filed
against the record they belong to rather than in folders. A document is only
useful next to the asset or the site it describes, which is why it is filed that
way.

One thing to know before anything else. **This demonstration records the
document, not the file.** The title, kind, file name, size, version, who filed
it and when are all real and searchable. The bytes of the file are not stored,
and every screen that shows a document says so.

## What is on the screen

**The page heading:** **Documents**, then "Manuals, certificates and drawings,
attached where they are needed."

**A toolbar**: a search box, "Title, file name or record", filter buttons,
**Columns**, **Clear filters**, and a count.

**The table**, newest first. Columns are Title, Kind, Filed against, Type, Size,
Version, and Uploaded.

**Kind** is one of Manual, Certificate, Drawing, Procedure, Contract, Report or
Photo.

**Filed against** names the record: "Asset" and its tag, "Work order" and its
number, "Plan" and its code, "Site" and its name, or **Organization** for a
document that belongs to the whole organization rather than one record.

## Find a document

1. Type into the search box — it matches the title, the file name and the
   record it is filed against.
2. Or filter by kind, by type, or by what it is filed against.
3. **Clear filters** starts again.

If nothing matches, **No matches** appears with a button to clear the filters.

## See the documents for one machine or one job

You usually want the paperwork for a specific thing, not the whole library.

- **For a machine:** open the asset and go to its **Documents** tab. Chapter 4.
- **For a job:** open the work order and look at **Attachments**. Chapter 5.

Both lists show the same details as the library: kind, file name, size, version
and the date filed.

## File a document

Documents are attached from the record they belong to, not from the library.

1. Open the work order or the asset.
2. Press **Attach a document**.
3. Choose the **Kind** — Manual, Certificate, Drawing, Procedure, Contract,
   Report or Photo.
4. Choose the file.
5. A message confirms the file name was recorded, and repeats that the file
   itself is not stored in this demonstration.

PDF, Word and Excel files are accepted. Anything else is refused with a message
naming the file and saying "this system takes PDF, Word and Excel files."

Picture: `docs/screenshots/WA-21/en-upload-refused-1440.png`

## What each role can do here

Every one of the six roles may open Documents and read the library. Like Assets
and Work orders, it is a screen everybody can see.

**Attaching a document** is held by the administrator, the facility manager, the
maintenance coordinator, the technician and the inspector. It reaches the
technician and the inspector on purpose: the person standing at the machine is
usually the person with the photograph, and paperwork that has to wait for a
coordinator arrives a day late or not at all.

An **auditor** may not attach anything. The **Attach a document** button stays
on screen, switched off, and says "Auditor cannot do this. Switch role to
continue."
